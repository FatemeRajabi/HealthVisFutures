About the Lab

At HealthVisFutures Lab, we design visualization and AI tools that help patients and clinicians make sense of complex health information. Our work addresses real-world problems across health data tracking, patient-provider communication, AI-assisted health information seeking, and clinical decision support.

We take a human-centred approach, working with patients and healthcare providers to build technologies that are meaningful, usable, and grounded in clinical reality. Our research is published at venues including IEEE VIS, CHI, and EuroVis.
