Join Our Lab

We are always looking for motivated undergraduate and graduate students to work with us in the areas of Information Visualization and Human-Computer Interaction focused on eHealth, Biomedical, and Public Health. Please feel free to look into our research projects.

If you are interested in working in these research areas, please contact Dr. Fateme Rajabiyazdi with a CV, your transcripts, and a statement of interest detailing your research interests, background, and past experience — or include Fateme's name in your application.

Graduate Students

We accept MASc and PhD students in Biomedical Engineering, Computer Science, and related fields. Strong interest in visualization, HCI, or health informatics is an asset.

Undergraduate Students

We welcome undergraduate researchers through co-op, NSERC USRA, or independent study. Relevant coursework in programming, design, or health sciences is helpful.

Postdoctoral Researchers

Prospective postdocs with a background in HCI, visualization, or health data are encouraged to reach out with a research statement and CV.

Research Collaborators

We collaborate with clinical partners, industry, and other academic groups. If you see alignment with our work, get in touch to explore possibilities.

Get in Touch

Email fateme.rajabiyazdi@ucalgary.ca with your CV, transcripts, and a short statement of interest. We look forward to hearing from you.
